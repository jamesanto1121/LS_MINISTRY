Sunday School &
Kids Club

We nurture young hearts in biblical truths through engaging lessons, joyful worship, and creative activities. Our curriculum is designed to help children understand God's love in age-appropriate ways.

  • Safe Nursery Care

    Secure environment for infants during service hours.

  • Interactive Bible Stories

    Crafts, memory verses, and fun learning for ages 5-12.

  • Vacation Bible School

    Summer camps filled with joy, friendship, and faith.

Women of Grace

Sisters journeying together through intercession, mentorship, and practical service. We meet monthly for Bible study, prayer chains, and social gatherings that strengthen our bonds in Christ.

  • Monthly Breakfast Meetings
  • Parenting Workshops
  • Charity Outreach Drives

Parish Choir

Leading the congregation in reverent hymns and contemporary songs honoring our Triune God. Our team comprises skilled musicians, vocalists, and technical volunteers who dedicate their talents to enhance the worship experience.
