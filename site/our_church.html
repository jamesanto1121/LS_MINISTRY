About Us

Who We Are.
A Family of Faith.

Grace Church Fellowship is a multi-generational parish community rooted steadfastly in the Holy Scriptures, bonded in warm Christian fellowship, and devoted to serving the city of Bengaluru with quiet compassion.

Vision & Mission

Growing in Grace,
Living in Love.

Our vision is to see every person in Bengaluru encounter the transforming love of Jesus Christ. We believe that church is not a building, but a family—a place where broken hearts are healed, doubts are answered, and faith is strengthened through genuine relationships.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of hope and truth in South India, raising disciples who make disciples.

Our Mission

To worship God authentically, serve our neighbors practically, and share the Gospel boldly.

Doctrine

Core Beliefs

We hold fast to these foundational truths derived from Scripture.

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God, the supreme authority in all matters of faith and conduct. It is sufficient, clear, and powerful to equip every believer for good works.
We worship one God eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. They are co-equal in power and glory, distinct yet united in essence and purpose.
Salvation is a free gift from God, received solely through faith in Jesus Christ’s atoning death and resurrection. Good works are the fruit of salvation, not the root.
The local church is the visible body of Christ on earth. We emphasize baptism, communion, corporate worship, and mutual accountability among believers as vital disciplines of the Christian life.
Journey

Our History

1994

The Beginning

Started as a small home group of 12 families in Indiranagar, driven by a desire for authentic biblical worship.

2005

First Sanctuary

Moved into our first dedicated building on Residency Road. Congregation grew to 150 members.

2015

Expansion & Outreach

Launched major community service initiatives, feeding thousands during drought seasons and starting schools.

Today

A Thriving Family

With multiple campuses online and offline, we continue to grow in grace and truth, serving Bengaluru with love.

Grace Church
